## From inside the app

Checkpoint64 has a feedback form built in. A report filed there becomes a **thread you can follow**:
replies show up against your report, and you can read the whole conversation under
**Settings → Feedback** in the app. If you have linked your Discord account, replies also arrive as
a DM. This is the fastest route for anything involving a specific save, because the report carries
the context we need.

## Community

- **Discord** — [discord.gg/kxeYwuuHEn](https://discord.gg/kxeYwuuHEn). The bot also handles account
  work: `/link` connects your account, then `/addsave`, `/usage`, `/status`, and `/requestgame`
  work without leaving Discord.
- **Missing a game?** `/requestgame` in Discord, or the in-app request form, adds it to the queue
  for a built-in preset. Any game that writes saves to a folder already works — a preset just
  removes the folder hunting.
- **GitHub** — release notes and downloads at
  [github.com/checkpoint64/checkpoint64/releases](https://github.com/checkpoint64/checkpoint64/releases).

## Security disclosure

Email [security@checkpoint64.com](mailto:security@checkpoint64.com) with enough detail to reproduce
the issue. Please give us a reasonable window to ship a fix before publishing. We do not run a paid
bounty programme, and we will not take legal action against anyone who reports in good faith,
avoids privacy violations and service degradation, and does not access or modify other people's
save data.
