HOW CHECKPOINT64 WORKS

POINT IT AT A FOLDER.
FORGET ABOUT IT.

SEE THE FULL WALKTHROUGH →
01 · UPLOAD

HIT UPLOAD ↑

Grab a snapshot of your save folder and send a copy to the cloud. Each upload becomes its own labelled version — version one of many.

02 · AUTO-BACKUP

FLIP AUTO ON

Checkpoint64 checks your save folder every 60 seconds. If something changed, it waits for the game to finish writing, then uploads a fresh version on its own. Only the changed files get sent — the rest is skipped.

03 · RESTORE

ROLL IT BACK

Click Versions → on any save to see every backup. Pick one and hit Restore — Checkpoint64 puts the files back and marks that version as the current one. Your 2am mistake becomes a 30-second fix.

WHAT'S IN THE BOX.

▮ VERSION HISTORY

EVERY UPLOAD IS A VERSION.

Scroll through every backup with file count, size, and what changed since last time. Hit Restore and the files go back on disk, marked as current — no guessing, no “final_v2_REAL” folders.

▮ CO-OP LOCKS

ONE PERSON HOLDS THE WORLD.

Games like Factorio, Valheim, and Satisfactory have one live world at a time. Whoever holds the lock uploads; everyone else downloads. Holder gone quiet? Locks expire on their own, and you can take over — with a warning, and a logbook entry so everyone knows.

▮ ONLY WHAT CHANGED

TINY UPLOADS.

Only the files that changed get uploaded — renamed files cost nothing extra. A 500 MB Minecraft world re-uploads as a few MB after a normal session, not the whole thing. Easy on your internet, easy on your storage.

▮ 140+ GAMES READY

SET UP IN SECONDS.

Presets for 140+ games — four flavours of modded Minecraft, Stardew, Skyrim, Palworld, Elden Ring — plus seven emulators. Pick which files count and skip the screenshots. If it writes saves to a folder, it works.

▮ SHARE CODES

ONE WORLD, WHOLE CROWD.

Running a community world? Mint a join code and anyone holding it can download your save — but never upload over it. Codes are capped and revocable, and read-only visitors don’t use up seats. Every plan can host — 3 fans at once on Free, 15 on Lifetime, unlimited on Pro.

▮ LOGBOOK

WHO DID WHAT, WHEN.

Every upload, restore, and lock-grab gets written down in your group’s logbook. Handy when your co-op partner blames you for the bad run.
